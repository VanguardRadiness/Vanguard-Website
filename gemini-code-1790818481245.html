Mandate Deadline: January 1, 2027

Colorado ADMT Readiness — January 1, 2027

Colorado has enacted groundbreaking legislation governing automated decision-making technologies (ADMT) involved in consequential decisions impacting consumers (including employment, housing, financial services, healthcare, and education).

Vanguard Readiness helps organizations identify potentially relevant systems, assess compliance readiness, document robust governance controls, and prepare for applicable statutory requirements before enforcement begins.

Important Regulatory Disclaimer: Regulatory requirements continue to evolve. Vanguard Readiness LLC provides governance, risk management, and compliance-readiness consulting services. We do not provide formal legal advice or legal representation.

Key Colorado ADMT Focus Areas

  • Consequential Decision Mapping Identifying algorithms impacting legal or similarly significant effects on consumer rights.
  • Risk Assessments & Impact Evaluations Documenting algorithmic bias testing, data provenance, and impact mitigations.
  • Consumer Notice & Opt-Out Protocols Establishing workflows for transparent consumer disclosures and human review channels.
  • Vendor & Third-Party Oversight Due diligence on procured AI models to ensure downstream compliance readiness.

Industry Standard Framework

NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF)

Vanguard uses the NIST AI RMF as a structured, defensible standard for helping organizations establish responsible, documented, risk-based AI governance.

G

GOVERN

Cultivating a culture of risk management. Establishing policies, accountability structures, diverse teams, and legal oversight.

M

MAP

Contextualizing risk. Identifying AI system characteristics, intended uses, stakeholders, and potential societal impacts.

M

MEASURE

Analyzing and tracking risk. Employing qualitative and quantitative tools to assess trustworthiness, bias, and performance.

M

MANAGE

Allocating resources and prioritizing risks. Implementing mitigation strategies, incident response, and continuous verification.
